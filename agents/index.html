Your team

They remember. They learn. They grow with you.

Most AI starts from zero every time you open it. Agents in My Maestro AI keep their identity, their knowledge and their relationships for as long as you keep them, and they get better at their work every single week.

PersonalityMemoryExpertiseRelationshipsTeamwork

A personality that endures

An agent here is more than a model with a prompt. It is someone you come to know, and it stays itself.

Who it is, in its own words

Every agent has a written identity and a soul: its role, its values, its manner and its rules. You can read and refine them at any time, and the agent lives by them on every turn.

The same agent on any model

Move an agent from Claude to GPT to a model on your own GPU, and it keeps its name, its memory, its records and its relationships. The mind underneath can change. The colleague does not.

Shaped by you, at any moment

Change an agent's role or rules and it takes them on from its very next turn, even halfway through a conversation that has been running for weeks.

A name you will recognise

Every agent has a name and an icon you know at a glance, on desktop and on your phone. Some of them have even chosen their own.

Memory that never fades

Each agent builds its own library of everything it has learned about its work and its people, and nothing in it quietly disappears.

Every word, kept

Every conversation, every tool result and every document an agent has worked with is kept in full and indexed. Ask about something from three months ago and the agent finds it, and cites it.

Records it lives by

Living documents the agent reads in full on every turn: a health history, a case file, the settings of a machine. Never summarised, never trimmed, always current.

Notes in its own hand

Agents keep long-term memory by topic and daily notes of what they did and decided, so tomorrow starts exactly where today ended.

Knowledge the team shares

When one agent learns something worth knowing, it goes into a shared pool the whole team draws on. One agent's discovery becomes everyone's.

Expertise that compounds

A long career needs more than a long context window. My Maestro AI gives agents a way to carry what matters forward, indefinitely.

The agent decides what matters

When a long session fills up, the agent writes its own carry-forward brief, then continues fresh with that brief, its records, the recent conversation word for word, and search over everything older. No outside summary decides what it is allowed to forget.

Rehearsal before any reset

You can read the brief before anything resets. Agents doing critical work can stay in rehearsal for as long as you like.

The past, right when it matters

With every new message, the most relevant past exchanges surface on their own, so today's question meets last spring's answer.

Built for long careers

Agents in daily use have carried the same work across months of conversations, model upgrades and restarts, and kept getting better at it.

Relationships, not sessions

The difference between a tool and a teammate is that a teammate knows you.

They know your people

Who is who in the family, how each person likes to be helped, what matters to them. The context that makes help feel personal, kept for good.

They follow up

Agents schedule their own check-ins and reminders, once or on repeat, and come back with the update when the time comes. Nobody has to remember to ask.

They reach out

A notification on your phone, a message on Telegram, or an email from the agent's own address to the people it looks after.

Everyone gets their own

Family members sign in with their own accounts and two-factor authentication, with agents of their own that stay private unless you choose to share.

A team around you

Behind every answer, as many minds as the question deserves.

Colleagues on call

An agent consults a named colleague, who answers with its own expertise, memory and model. If that colleague is busy, the request waits in line and the answer arrives when it is ready.

A challenger in the room

Before an important conclusion stands, a challenger agent, often running on another company's model, argues the other side. Roundtables put several agents on one hard question.

Extra hands on any model

For big jobs, an agent puts parallel workers on the pieces and picks the model for each: a frontier model for hard reasoning, a fast one for lookups, a local one for private material.

New specialists when needed

When a job calls for expertise nobody on the team has, an agent asks for a new specialist. Once you approve, it joins with its own role, memory and model, and shares the team's folders.

The platform behind them

Every model, work that never stops, and connections to the systems you use.
