Before you install

Powerful agents need a trusted home.

My Maestro AI runs on your computer and works through the AI models and accounts you connect. That's what makes it useful, and it's also what your security depends on. We recommend it for you and people you trust, with agents you trust, and with nothing in it you'd mind a third party seeing or a breach exposing.

Read the security overview →
  • Your host computer. Keeping your Maestro safe depends on the computer it runs on: its updates, its accounts and who can use it.
  • Your AI providers. A cloud model sees what your agents send it.
  • What agents can reach. Email, files and connected accounts give agents real reach.
