Read the security overview →

My Maestro AI runs on your computer and works through the AI models and accounts you connect. That's what makes it useful, and it's also what your security depends on. We recommend it for you and people you trust, with agents you trust, and with nothing in it you'd mind a third party seeing or a breach exposing.

Your agents

More about your team →

A personality that endures

Each agent has a character, values and a way of working, written down and lived by on every turn. Change the model underneath and it is still the same agent, with the same history and the same commitments to you.

Personalities →

Expertise that compounds

Agents keep their own records, their own notes and a complete, searchable history of every conversation and every document they have read. Every week on the job, they know their field and your situation better.

Memory →

Relationships

They know who is who, what you prefer and what worried you last month. They check in when they said they would, answer the people they look after, and reach your phone when something needs you.

Relationships →

A team that works together

Agents consult each other, bring in specialists, call a challenger to argue the other side, and put extra workers on any model to run in parallel. You talk to one, and the whole team gets to work.

An example: a family health agent

A family health agent, a few months into the job, getting ready for an appointment. None of what it says was in the question. It comes from the agent's records, its memory and a quick consult with a colleague.

  • Every reading, prescription and visit note stays in the agent's records, on your own machine.
  • Specialist agents are one consult away: pharmacy, cardiology, genetics and more.
  • It remembers how each person likes to be helped, and it follows up on its own.

How teams work →

Each agent is an expert in one area and part of something bigger. A few of the teams already at work:

Family health

A health lead with a bench of specialist agents, from cardiology and oncology to pharmacy and genetics. Every record organised, every appointment prepared, a second opinion always on call.

Legal and estate

Estate planning, probate and document drafting, with a reviewer that checks every draft before it reaches you.

Household

Appliances, contractors, warranties, bills and the smart home, all remembered, so the same problem never costs you twice.

Trading desk

A strategist backed by independent auditors that rebuild every backtest and verify every contract, behind a wallet firewall that enforces your limits.

Workshop

An expert on your exact machines: their manuals, settings and quirks, from a CNC router's setup to a tricky repair.

Security

A security analyst that reviews your systems, tracks new vulnerabilities and audits the hub itself.

Any model, the same agent

Run each agent on Claude, GPT, Gemini or Perplexity, through your subscription or an API key, or on open models running on your own GPU. Memory, records and tools are identical on every one, so you can move to a better model the day it arrives.

What stays on your computer

Your agents, their memories and every conversation live on your own computer. Hosted models see only the turns they handle, and local models keep everything in the house. You decide what each agent may do, and nothing risky happens without the permission you set.
