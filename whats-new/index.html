What's new

New in My Maestro AI over the last two weeks.

Talk to your team

Clearer chats, easier answers and times you can trust.

Question cards are easier to answer

The answer box grows as you type, and Return starts a new line. Skip is now a button beside Submit, so you can pass on a question in one tap.

Calm, folded cards in the chat

When your Maestro starts something on its own, such as a consultation or a scheduled task, it shows as one small closed card. Tap it to read everything.

A time under every message

See when you sent each message and when each reply finished, and how long it took. Older conversations get times too.

Agents know your local time

Agents now read the time in your own time zone, so “this morning” means your morning.

The chat uses the whole window

Messages and the message box now fill the width of your screen instead of sitting in a narrow column.

Answers stay in view

Answered questions and finished workers no longer pile up under a long reply, and an answer given minutes later still shows the rest of the reply.

Your data, safely

Backups that protect everything your Maestro knows.

Encrypted backups of everything

One backup holds your settings, agents, memories, chats and files. It is locked with a backup password you choose.

Nightly backups

Once backups are on, one is made every night, and your Maestro keeps the recent ones. You are told if a night is missed.

A copy in your Google Drive

Each nightly backup can be copied to a folder in your Google Drive. It can see only the files it puts there, and backups are encrypted before they leave your computer.

Restore with a warning first

Before anything changes, your Maestro shows what will be replaced and asks you to confirm. Undo is available for 7 days.

A new password or key when you need one

Change the backup password at any time, or move your backups to a new key if one may have leaked.

Connect your world

Data connectors bring your own accounts and devices into your Maestro.

Ring cameras

Your Maestro can receive motion and doorbell events, save a picture of each and tell the agent you choose. That agent can also ask for the latest or a live picture.

Oura Ring

Your agents can read your sleep, readiness, activity, heart rate and workouts when you ask. It is read-only, and only your lead agent can see it unless you choose others.

Google Drive

Keep a copy of every backup in your own Drive. A guided setup walks you through it, and it takes about 15 minutes once.

Remote computers

Set a computer aside for your agents. You decide how far each one may go: look, act, or take over the mouse and keyboard. Your phone asks first.

Guided setup, private by default

Modules are now called Data connectors, with a guided setup. A new connector is open to your lead agent only until you pick others.

Agents that work for you

Build a team more easily and keep an eye on how it is doing.

Maestro builds agents with you

Ask for a new agent and Maestro asks what it is for and who will use it. For a personality, it offers a name, icon and voice you can keep or change, then tests the agent before handing it over.

One Workers list

See every worker that is running, which agent started it, which model it uses and how long it has run. Open one to follow along, or stop it.

Choose how many workers run at once

Set a limit for each AI service. If a service pushes back, your Maestro slows down and builds back up on its own.

A memory check for every agent

Each night your Maestro checks how every agent’s memory is doing and says so in plain words. A button next to each finding fixes it.

Claude Fable 5.2

Agents can now run on Claude Fable 5.2, and it appears in the list of models you can choose.

ChatGPT subscription

Connect your paid ChatGPT plan during setup, next to Claude, and its models show up in the pickers.

Security

More ways to keep your Maestro locked down.

Unlock the wallet with Face ID or a fingerprint

After a restart you no longer need to type the wallet passphrase. Add your phone or computer once, then unlock with your face, fingerprint or screen lock.

Two-factor for admins

A new setting, on by default, requires two-factor sign-in for admins. Turning it off asks for your password and a code, and every change is recorded.

Node.js security update

New installs include the latest Node.js security fixes. Older installs are warned, with the exact fix to apply.

Other agents’ history is guarded

An agent running on your computer can no longer read the searchable history of other agents’ conversations.

Choose each agent’s computer access

See and change what each agent may do on your computers, right beside its name. Asking before each session stays on unless an admin turns it off.

On your phone

Small fixes that make the phone page nicer to use.

Fixing a typo keeps the keyboard open

Tapping a misspelled word no longer hides the keyboard, so you can correct it in place.

Tables fit the screen

Wide tables in replies now wrap to the width of your phone, so you no longer scroll sideways to read them.

A menu that scrolls

On a short screen, the ⋮ menu now scrolls, so every item, including Sign out, can be reached.

Activity moved to the menu

The Activity button now lives in the ⋮ menu, so it is not tapped by accident. The bottom bar keeps Agents and Chat.

Notifications open in your tab

Clicking a notification in a desktop browser opens it in the tab you already have.

Every change is in the full release notes. To get these updates, see Update your Maestro.
