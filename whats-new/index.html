5.12.0 · November 20, 2026

On the stable channel. What’s new since 5.11.0.

Choose how your Maestro looks

Three interface styles, chosen on each device in Settings → Appearance or the phone's menu: Engineering, Broadsheet House (warm paper and dark ink) and Quiet Page (a calm light notebook). Every page and Simple mode follow the style you pick.

Spend figures count only what you pay

The spend figure beside your agents and in Settings counts only pay-per-use calls through API keys and paid cloud voices. Your Claude and ChatGPT plans no longer show as dollars spent, and daily spend caps count real spend only.

Hand long work to another agent

Your agent can pass a long job, such as development or a long piece of writing, to another agent and stay free to talk with you. A line above the message box shows how it is going. Build partners, who take such work by default, wait for your decision on existing installs.

A nightly health check of your computer

Every night and after each start, your Maestro checks the computer it runs on: free disk space, memory, backups, the Claude and ChatGPT sign-ins and waiting security updates. A serious problem, such as a nearly full disk, sends a notice to the admins’ phones.

Your Maestro's own files stay safe

An update stops and lists program files that were changed on your computer instead of breaking. You can also stop agents from changing your Maestro's own code; on existing installs this waits for your decision.

Tiger Teams on the phone

Admins find Tiger Teams in the phone's menu, with each member's current step and Stop at hand. A member that needs your approval asks on the team page, rounds are counted, and the pages are safer.

See how much of your plans is used

Settings → Subscriptions shows how much of each Claude and ChatGPT plan is used and when it resets, in your local time.

Your agents say what they are doing

While an agent works on something you didn't start, such as a hand-off or a scheduled task, its chat header and its line in the agent list say what it is doing.

Also in 5.12.0

  • Your lead agent suggests newer models when they become available; nothing switches by itself.
  • Agents pick temporary workers from today's newest models.
  • Hand-offs take one line above the message box.
  • Your Maestro keeps Codex current and tells you when the Claude client or a local engine has an update.
  • A tidier phone menu, with the interface style on top.
  • Reasoning no longer shows up as reply text after a dropped connection.
  • Members no longer receive admin-only notices when their page reconnects.
  • Home folder sizes read as “5 GB” rather than “5120 MB”.

5.11.0 · November 20, 2026

On the stable channel. What’s new since 5.10.3.

Talk with your agents, hands-free

Start a voice call and just talk: your agent answers aloud, and you can cut in at any time. The voices are free and run on your own computer. Choose an animated character, a glowing orb or the chat on screen, use your car or Bluetooth headset, and keep recordings of calls if you switch them on.

Simple mode

A calm view for everyday use: your agents as a list of chats and one message box, with a reasoning level in plain words, the microphone and voice calls.

Download, share and sort files

Share a file with a short link that works for an hour, a day or a week. Download a whole folder as a zip. The Files panel lists the newest files first and sorts by name or size.

Your other computers, from your phone

For a computer you have connected, Open desktop on the Remote computers page shows its live screen in your browser. Use it with your own touch, mouse and keyboard, and pinch to zoom on a phone.

A team from the first day

New installs offer a small starter team next to your lead agent: a Challenger that checks important work and three helpers. Every agent that hands out work is taught to run a team, and your lead can suggest new workers when the same kind of work keeps coming back.

Speed and price per agent

Admins choose Standard, Fast or Economy for each agent: Fast answers sooner for a higher price, Economy costs less and suits scheduled and background work.

Your agents are checked every day

After every start and each night, your Maestro has test agents read, write, search and run a command the way your agents do, and tells you when something stops working.

Changes to how agents behave wait for you

When an update changes how agents behave, an install that already has agents keeps the change switched off. Your lead agent explains what it would mean and what it recommends, and you decide.

Also in 5.11.0

  • Choose your notifications, agent by agent.
  • Hand an agent a picture while it is working.
  • Agents that are working show at the top of the agent list.
  • Your agents' commands no longer see your Maestro's secrets, and your Claude sign-in never goes into an agent's box.
  • Two-factor codes lock for a minute after five wrong tries.
  • Restart and the clean-up of stuck commands work on a Mac installed with the installer.

Talk to your team

Clearer chats, easier answers and times you can trust.

Question cards are easier to answer

The answer box grows as you type, and Return starts a new line. Skip is now a button beside Submit, so you can pass on a question in one tap.

Simpler chats with folded cards

When your Maestro starts something on its own, such as a consultation or a scheduled task, it shows as one small closed card. Tap it to read everything.

A time under every message

See when you sent each message and when each reply finished, and how long it took. Older conversations get times too.

Agents know your local time

Agents now read the time in your own time zone, so “this morning” means your morning.

The chat uses the whole window

Messages and the message box now fill the width of your screen instead of sitting in a narrow column.

Answers stay in view

Answered questions and finished workers no longer pile up under a long reply, and an answer given minutes later still shows the rest of the reply.

Your data, safely

Backups that protect everything your Maestro knows.

Encrypted backups of everything

One backup holds your settings, agents, memories, chats and files. It is locked with a backup password you choose.

Nightly backups

Once backups are on, one is made every night, and your Maestro keeps the recent ones. You are told if a night is missed.

A copy in your Google Drive

Each nightly backup can be copied to a folder in your Google Drive. It can see only the files it puts there, and backups are encrypted before they leave your computer.

Restore with a warning first

Before anything changes, your Maestro shows what will be replaced and asks you to confirm. Undo is available for 7 days.

A new password or key when you need one

Change the backup password at any time, or move your backups to a new key if one may have leaked.

Connect your world

Data connectors bring your own accounts and devices into your Maestro.

Ring cameras

Your Maestro can receive motion and doorbell events, save a picture of each and tell the agent you choose. That agent can also ask for the latest or a live picture.

Oura Ring

Your agents can read your sleep, readiness, activity, heart rate and workouts when you ask. It is read-only, and only your lead agent can see it unless you choose others.

Google Drive

Keep a copy of every backup in your own Drive. A guided setup walks you through it, and it takes about 15 minutes once.

Remote computers

Set a computer aside for your agents. You decide how far each one may go: look, act, or take over the mouse and keyboard. Your phone asks first.

Guided setup, private by default

Modules are now called Data connectors, with a guided setup. A new connector is open to your lead agent only until you pick others.

Agents that work for you

Build a team more easily and keep an eye on how it is doing.

Maestro builds agents with you

Ask for a new agent and Maestro asks what it is for and who will use it. For a personality, it offers a name, icon and voice you can keep or change, then tests the agent before handing it over.

One Workers list

See every worker that is running, which agent started it, which model it uses and how long it has run. Open one to follow along, or stop it.

Choose how many workers run at once

Set a limit for each AI service. If a service pushes back, your Maestro slows down and builds back up on its own.

A memory check for every agent

Each night your Maestro checks how every agent’s memory is doing and says so in plain words. A button next to each finding fixes it.

Claude Fable 5.2

Agents can now run on Claude Fable 5.2, and it appears in the list of models you can choose.

ChatGPT subscription

Connect your paid ChatGPT plan during setup, next to Claude, and its models show up in the pickers.

Security

More ways to keep your Maestro locked down.

Unlock the wallet with Face ID or a fingerprint

After a restart you no longer need to type the wallet passphrase. Add your phone or computer once, then unlock with your face, fingerprint or screen lock.

Two-factor for admins

A new setting, on by default, requires two-factor sign-in for admins. Turning it off asks for your password and a code, and every change is recorded.

Node.js security update

New installs include the latest Node.js security fixes. Older installs are warned, with the exact fix to apply.

Other agents’ history is guarded

An agent running on your computer can no longer read the searchable history of other agents’ conversations.

Choose each agent’s computer access

See and change what each agent may do on your computers, right beside its name. Asking before each session stays on unless an admin turns it off.

On your phone

Small fixes that make the phone page nicer to use.

Fixing a typo keeps the keyboard open

Tapping a misspelled word no longer hides the keyboard, so you can correct it in place.

Tables fit the screen

Wide tables in replies now wrap to the width of your phone, so you no longer scroll sideways to read them.

A menu that scrolls

On a short screen, the ⋮ menu now scrolls, so every item, including Sign out, can be reached.

Activity moved to the menu

The Activity button now lives in the ⋮ menu, so it is not tapped by accident. The bottom bar keeps Agents and Chat.

Notifications open in your tab

Clicking a notification in a desktop browser opens it in the tab you already have.
